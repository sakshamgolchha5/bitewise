Bitewise privacy notice

For the initial tester build. Bitewise stores your food log, profile, saved meals, corrections and preferences in your browser on your device. Clearing the site's data removes that local information.

When you use AI
Photos and text you choose to analyse are sent to the Bitewise server, which forwards them to Google Gemini. The Bitewise server does not intentionally store or log those photos or prompts.

Barcode lookups: product barcode searches are sent to Open Food Facts so the app can retrieve packaged-food information.

Custom-food estimates: Bitewise may use Open Food Facts and Google Search grounding to improve nutrition estimates. Results are estimates and should be checked before logging or relying on them.

No accounts/analytics in this tester build: there is no Bitewise account system or analytics database in this version.

Health information: the app can contain food, weight and nutrition information you enter. Treat the tester link as private and share it only with people who understand this data flow.
